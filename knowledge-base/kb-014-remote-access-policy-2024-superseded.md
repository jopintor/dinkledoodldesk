# Remote Access Policy 2024 (SUPERSEDED)

> **This document is no longer in effect.** It was replaced on 1 March 2026 by KB-013 (HubConnect VPN for Branch Managers). It is kept only for audit history. Do not use it to answer current questions.

## Summary of the old rules

The 2024 policy covered the earlier VPN service, called **PH-Remote**, which was retired in February 2026.

- Any full-time employee could request remote access with approval from their immediate supervisor.
- Personal laptops were allowed if they had antivirus software installed.
- PH-Remote used a separate 8-digit "VPN PIN" instead of StaffID, and did not require MFA.
- Split tunnelling was enabled, so only head-office traffic went through the VPN.
- Sessions stayed open for up to 24 hours.

## Why it was replaced

An internal review in late 2025 found that most PH-Remote accounts were unused, that shared VPN PINs were common at two branches, and that personal laptops could not be checked for updates. The 2026 HubConnect rules fixed these by limiting access to managers and IT, requiring company laptops, using StaffID with MFA, and sending all traffic through the head office.
