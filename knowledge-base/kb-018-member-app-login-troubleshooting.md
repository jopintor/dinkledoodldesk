# PickleHub Member App Login Problems

Members sign in to the PickleHub Member App (iOS, Android and `member.picklehub.example`) with the **email address and password** they registered with, or with **Sign in with Google** if they set up their account that way. Member App accounts are separate from staff StaffID accounts.

## Forgot password

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address on your membership.
3. Open the reset email and tap the link. The link works for **30 minutes** and only once.
4. Choose a new password with at least 8 characters, including a number.
5. Sign in again on the app and on the club Wi-Fi.

No reset email? Check your Spam or Promotions folder, and make sure you typed the same email you used when you joined. Wait 5 minutes before requesting another link; each new link cancels the previous one.

## "Account locked"

After **5 wrong passwords**, the account locks for **30 minutes**. Wait the 30 minutes, then sign in or use **Forgot password?** Repeated lockouts with no wrong attempts on your side may mean someone else is trying your account; tell the front desk so staff can raise a ticket.

## Signed up with Google, now asked for a password

If you created your account with **Sign in with Google**, keep using that button. If you want a normal password too, use **Forgot password?** with your Gmail address to create one.

## "Email not found"

- Check for typing mistakes, or try another email you might have used.
- Members who joined at the front desk before June 2024 were moved to the app using the email on their paper form. Ask the front desk to confirm the email on your membership record.
- Front-desk staff may confirm the email only after checking the member's ID at the counter. They must never read out or send a member's email to someone over the phone or chat.

## The app shows a blank screen or keeps loading

1. Check the status page (`status.picklehub.example`) for **Member App**.
2. Update the app from the App Store or Google Play.
3. Close the app fully and open it again.
4. Try the website `member.picklehub.example`. If the website works, reinstall the app.

## Changing the email on your account

For security, members cannot change their sign-in email in the app. Visit the front desk with a valid ID; staff submit the change in CourtBook Admin.

## For staff helping a member

- Never ask a member for their password, and never sign in to the app as the member.
- You cannot see or reset a member's password. Guide them through **Forgot password?**
- If a member's issue is not solved by these steps, raise a ticket with their **member ID** (not their phone number or email) and a description of the error.
