# Accounts for New Hires and Departing Staff

## New hires (joiners)

### What happens automatically

When HR enters a new employee's start date, IT receives an onboarding ticket. Two business days before the start date, IT creates:

- A **StaffID** account (username `firstname.lastname`; a number is added if the name is already taken)
- A **HubMail** mailbox
- A temporary password, sent to the personal email address the new hire gave HR

On the first day, the new hire signs in and sets up a password and MFA (KB-004).

### What the manager must request

Access to business systems is **not** automatic. The branch manager submits access requests (KB-010) for:

- CourtBook Admin role (Front Desk or Coach)
- DinkPOS Cashier ID, if the person will handle payments
- Branch shared mailbox
- RallyBracket, if the person will help at tournaments

Submit these at least 3 business days before the start date so everything is ready on day one.

### Equipment

Company laptops are issued only to managers and head-office staff. Front-desk staff use the shared branch tablets with their own logins. Never sign in to a shared tablet with someone else's account.

## Departing staff (leavers)

### Manager's responsibilities

1. Tell HR and IT the last working day as soon as it is known.
2. On the last day, collect company laptops, keys and access cards.
3. Confirm in the offboarding ticket that equipment has been returned.

### What IT disables

At the end of the last working day, IT disables:

- StaffID (which also removes HubMail, CourtBook Admin, staff Wi-Fi and VPN)
- DinkPOS Cashier ID
- HubWatch, RallyBracket and any other separate accounts

If the person is leaving on bad terms or there is a security concern, the manager or HR should ask for **immediate** disabling by calling extension 4357. This is handled as P2.

### Mailbox after departure

The departed employee's HubMail is kept for 90 days. The manager can request forwarding of business emails to another staff member through a ticket. Personal messages are not forwarded.

## Changing roles or branches (movers)

When someone moves to a new role or branch, the old access is removed and the new access is requested through KB-010. Access does not carry over automatically.
