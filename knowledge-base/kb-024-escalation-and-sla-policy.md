# IT Incident Priority, SLA and Escalation Policy

## 1. Priority levels

Every ticket gets a priority based on how many people are affected and whether the clubs can keep operating.

| Priority | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 – Critical** | A branch cannot take payments or bookings, or a security incident is in progress | DinkPOS down on all tablets at an open branch; CourtBook down for everyone; confirmed account takeover |
| **P2 – High** | A major function is down, or many users are affected, but there is a workaround | Card payments down but cash works; staff Wi-Fi down at one branch; VPN down for all managers; tournament scoring blocked on event day |
| **P3 – Medium** | One user or a small feature is affected | One person cannot sign in; an access request; a single double booking; printer not working |
| **P4 – Low** | Questions, how-to requests and improvement ideas | "How do I export a report?"; feature suggestions |

## 2. Service level targets

Response means a person from IT has acknowledged the ticket and started work. Resolution targets are goals, not guarantees; complex vendor issues may take longer, and the ticket will say why.

| Priority | First response | Resolution target | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 15 minutes | 4 hours | 24/7 (on-call engineer after hours) |
| P2 | 1 hour | 8 service hours | Service Desk hours (7:00 AM – 10:00 PM) |
| P3 | 4 service hours | 2 business days | Service Desk hours |
| P4 | 1 business day | 5 business days | Service Desk hours |

Requests made through DinkDesk get an immediate automatic reply; the targets above apply to the human response.

## 3. Escalation levels

| Level | Who | Handles |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | DinkDesk (AI) | Answers from the knowledge base, StaffID password reset, ticket creation, service status checks |
| L1 | Service Desk Agents | Account issues, access requests, first troubleshooting |
| L2 | Systems and Network Administrator; Applications Specialist | Network, VPN, CCTV, CourtBook, DinkPOS, Member App and vendor issues |
| L3 | IT Lead and vendors | Major incidents, design changes, vendor escalations |

## 4. When DinkDesk hands over to a person

DinkDesk creates a ticket and passes the conversation to the Service Desk when:

1. The user asks to speak to a person.
2. DinkDesk is not confident its answer is correct, or the knowledge base does not cover the question.
3. The request needs approval or a manual action DinkDesk cannot do.
4. The issue looks like P1 or P2.
5. The topic is sensitive: security incidents, personal data breaches, CCTV footage, or a complaint about a staff member.

## 5. Escalating a ticket that is late

1. If the first response target has passed, reply to the ticket or ask DinkDesk to "escalate my ticket". The ticket moves to the next level.
2. If a P1 or P2 is still not resolved after its target, the IT Lead is notified automatically.
3. If you are still not satisfied, a branch manager can contact the IT Lead, Ramon Villareal, and then the Operations Head, Grace Lim.

## 6. After-hours P1 procedure

Between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM, call the on-call engineer at +63 900 000 4357 (fictional) **and** ask DinkDesk to create the ticket. Only P1 incidents are handled after hours; everything else is picked up when the Service Desk opens.
