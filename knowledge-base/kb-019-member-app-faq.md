# PickleHub Member App FAQ

Answers to common member questions about the PickleHub Member App. Problems signing in are covered in more detail in KB-018.

## How do I reset my Member App password?

Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and follow the link sent to your email. The link expires after 30 minutes. Full steps are in KB-018.

## My account is locked. What now?

Wait 30 minutes. The app locks accounts for 30 minutes after 5 wrong password attempts.

## How far ahead can I book a court?

Members can book up to **7 days** ahead. Bookings open at **12:00 midnight** each day for the date 7 days later. Premium members can book up to **14 days** ahead.

## How do I cancel a booking?

Go to **My Bookings**, tap the booking and choose **Cancel**. Cancellations made at least **24 hours** before the start time return the full amount as app credit. Later cancellations follow the club's fee rules shown in the app; the front desk handles questions about fees.

## I paid but my booking does not show.

Pull down on **My Bookings** to refresh. If the booking is still missing after 10 minutes, do not book again. Show the payment confirmation to the front desk or report it through the app's **Help > Report a problem** with the booking reference (it starts with `BK-`). See KB-020.

## Someone else is on the court I booked.

Show your booking in the app to the front desk. Staff will check CourtBook, which is the official record. See KB-020 for how double bookings are handled.

## Can I use the app on more than one phone?

Yes. You can be signed in on up to 3 devices. Club Wi-Fi allows 2 devices at a time (KB-012).

## Does the app work at all branches?

Yes. Choose the branch at the top of the **Book** screen. Your membership works at all five branches, but some clinics are only for members of a specific home branch.

## How do I delete my account or get a copy of my data?

Send a request to the Data Protection Officer at `dpo@picklehub.example`, or ask the front desk for the data request form. Account deletion is handled under the Data Privacy Policy (KB-023) and is not done in the app.

## Who do I contact for app problems?

Use **Help > Report a problem** in the app, ask DinkDesk, or speak to the front desk. App issues are handled by the IT team during Service Desk hours (7:00 AM – 10:00 PM).
