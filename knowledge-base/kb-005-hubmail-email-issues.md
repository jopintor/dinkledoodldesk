# HubMail Staff Email Problems

HubMail is PickleHub's staff email and calendar, hosted on Google Workspace. You sign in with your StaffID.

## Cannot sign in to HubMail

1. Check the status page (`status.picklehub.example`) for a HubMail incident. If one is listed, wait for the update; no ticket is needed (KB-025).
2. Make sure you are using your StaffID username in the form `firstname.lastname@picklehub.example`.
3. If the password is rejected, see KB-001 to reset it, or KB-003 if the account is locked.
4. If the MFA step fails, see KB-004.

## Email stopped syncing on my phone

This usually happens right after a password reset.

1. Open the mail app on your phone.
2. Remove the HubMail account and add it again, or tap the "Sign in again" notice.
3. Complete the MFA prompt.

## I am not receiving emails from members or partners

1. Check the **Spam** folder.
2. Ask the sender to confirm they used the correct address.
3. If emails from a known partner keep going to spam, raise a ticket with the sender's address and the date of one missing email. Do not forward the whole member email thread into the ticket if it contains personal data; a date and sender address are enough.

## Shared mailboxes

Each branch has a shared front-desk mailbox, for example `das.frontdesk@picklehub.example`.

- Access is granted by the branch manager through an access request (KB-010).
- Shared mailboxes are opened from your own HubMail account. They do not have their own password, so never ask for "the front-desk email password".

## Mailbox is full

Staff mailboxes have 30 GB of storage. Delete large attachments you no longer need, and empty Trash. If you still need more space for work reasons, raise a ticket.

## Sending member data by email

Do not email member lists, payment details or ID photos to personal accounts or outside parties. See the Data Privacy Policy (KB-023).
