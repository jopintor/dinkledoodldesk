# Reporting Phishing and Security Incidents

## What to report

Report anything that could put PickleHub accounts, devices or member data at risk, for example:

- An email, SMS or chat message asking you to sign in, pay, or share a code, especially if it is urgent or unexpected
- MFA prompts or password reset codes you did not request
- Someone calling as "IT" or "the bank" asking for passwords, PINs or access to a terminal
- A lost or stolen company laptop, tablet or phone with HubMail
- Strange activity on a POS tablet or card terminal, such as an unknown device attached to it
- Member data sent to the wrong person or left visible in public

When in doubt, report it. You will never get in trouble for a report that turns out to be harmless.

## How to report

1. Email `security@picklehub.example`, or ask DinkDesk to raise a **security incident** ticket. DinkDesk will pass it straight to the Service Desk.
2. For a suspicious email, use **Report phishing** in HubMail (the flag icon). This sends a copy to IT. Do not forward it to colleagues to ask their opinion.
3. If it is happening right now (for example, someone is tampering with a card terminal or you clicked a link and entered your password), also call the Service Desk on extension 4357, or the on-call engineer after hours. This is P1.
4. If member personal data may be involved, also tell the Data Protection Officer (`dpo@picklehub.example`) — see KB-023.

## If you clicked a phishing link or entered your password

1. Reset your StaffID password immediately (KB-001), from a device you trust.
2. Report it as above and say what you entered and when.
3. Watch for MFA prompts you did not start and deny them.
4. IT will check your sign-in history and sign out other sessions.

## Signs of a phishing message

- The sender's address does not end in `@picklehub.example`, or is a near-copy such as `@picklehub-example.com`
- Urgent threats ("your account will be deleted in 1 hour")
- Links where the address does not match the text shown
- Requests for gift cards, e-wallet transfers or changes to bank details
- Attachments you were not expecting, especially `.zip`, `.html` or files asking you to "enable content"

## What IT does next

The Systems and Network Administrator reviews every report, blocks malicious senders and websites, and resets affected accounts. You will get a reply on the ticket. Confirmed incidents involving personal data are handled together with the DPO.

## Do not

- Do not reply to, forward, or "test" a suspicious link.
- Do not try to investigate an attacker yourself.
- Do not delete the message before reporting it.
