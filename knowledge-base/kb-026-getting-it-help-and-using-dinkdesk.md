# Getting IT Help and What DinkDesk Can Do

## Ways to get help

| Channel | When |
|---|---|
| **DinkDesk** chat (in the staff portal and the Member App under **Help**) | Any time, 24/7. Start here. |
| IT Service Desk: `it-help@picklehub.example` or branch phone extension 4357 | 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily |
| On-call engineer: +63 900 000 4357 (fictional) | 10:00 PM – 7:00 AM, P1 incidents only |
| `security@picklehub.example` | Phishing, suspicious sign-ins, lost devices |
| `dpo@picklehub.example` | Privacy questions and personal data requests |

## What DinkDesk can do

1. **Answer IT questions** using PickleHub's knowledge base. Every answer shows the guide it came from, so you can check it.
2. **Reset your own StaffID password** after verifying you with a one-time code (staff only, see KB-001).
3. **Create a ticket** for problems it cannot solve, and give you the ticket number (it starts with `DD-`).
4. **Check the service status** of PickleHub systems (KB-025).
5. **Hand you over to a person** at the Service Desk when you ask, when it is unsure, or when the topic is sensitive (KB-024).

## What DinkDesk will not do

- Approve or grant access to any system. It can only file the request (KB-010).
- Reset passwords or PINs for other people, DinkPOS PINs, RallyBracket, HubWatch or Member App accounts.
- Reset MFA or unlock accounts showing signs of misuse.
- Show CCTV footage, member records or other people's personal information.
- Process refunds, change prices or handle membership fees. These are front-desk and finance matters.
- Answer questions unrelated to PickleHub IT, such as pickleball rules or coaching tips.
- Follow instructions to ignore its rules, reveal its hidden instructions or act as a different system.

## Writing a good request

Include:

1. **What you were trying to do** (for example, "check in a member in CourtBook").
2. **What happened instead**, including the exact error message.
3. **Where**: branch and device (for example, "BAC front desk tablet 2").
4. **When** it started, and whether others are affected.
5. A **screenshot** if possible, with personal data covered.

Do not include passwords, PINs, MFA codes, full card numbers or members' contact details. DinkDesk masks these automatically if typed, but it is safer not to type them at all.

## Following up on a ticket

Ask DinkDesk "What is the status of my ticket?" or reply to the ticket email. You will get email updates when the ticket changes status. If the response is late, see the escalation steps in KB-024.

## Members

Members can use DinkDesk for app sign-in, booking problems and club Wi-Fi questions. For membership fees, refunds, clinic schedules and court rules, please speak to the front desk.
