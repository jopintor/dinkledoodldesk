# Data Privacy Policy for Staff and IT Systems

## 1. Why this policy exists

PickleHub Clubs collects personal information about members, guests and employees. In the Philippines, the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)** protects this information, and the **National Privacy Commission (NPC)** enforces it. This policy explains, in plain terms, how staff must handle personal data in PickleHub's IT systems. It is a summary for daily work, not legal advice.

## 2. What counts as personal data

Any information that can identify a person, for example:

- Member names, mobile numbers, email addresses, birthdays and member IDs
- Booking history and attendance records
- Photos of IDs, CCTV video and photos taken at events
- Payment details, such as the last 4 digits of a card or e-wallet references
- Employee records

Some information is **sensitive personal information** and needs extra care, such as government ID numbers, health information (for example, injuries reported at a clinic) and age.

## 3. Principles staff must follow

The Act is built on three general principles. At PickleHub they mean:

| Principle | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Transparency** | Members are told what we collect and why, through the Member Privacy Notice in the app and at the front desk. |
| **Legitimate purpose** | Use data only for the purpose it was collected for, such as bookings, payments, safety and member service. |
| **Proportionality** | Collect and show only what is needed. For example, use the member ID in a ticket instead of the full name and phone number. |

## 4. Rules for IT systems

1. Access personal data only when your job needs it, and only through approved systems (CourtBook, DinkPOS, HubMail, HubWatch).
2. Do not copy member lists to personal devices, USB drives or personal cloud storage.
3. Do not put personal data in ticket titles or DinkDesk chats unless it is needed to solve the problem. DinkDesk automatically masks phone numbers, emails and card numbers, but you should still avoid typing them.
4. Never type full card numbers or government ID numbers into any system other than the one designed for them.
5. CCTV footage is released only through the DPO (KB-009).

## 5. Members' rights

Members have rights under the Act, including the right to be informed, to access their data, to have errors corrected, to object, and to request deletion in certain cases. Any request of this kind, including "delete my account" or "send me a copy of my data", goes to the **Data Protection Officer** at `dpo@picklehub.example`. Front-desk staff and DinkDesk do not process these requests themselves; they pass them on.

## 6. Personal data breaches

A breach is any loss, theft or unauthorised access to personal data, for example a lost laptop with member lists, an email sent to the wrong person, or someone seeing data they should not.

1. Report it **immediately** to the DPO (`dpo@picklehub.example`) and `security@picklehub.example`. Do not wait until you are sure.
2. Do not try to investigate or cover it up yourself.
3. The DPO assesses the breach. When the law requires it, PickleHub notifies the NPC and the affected people within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach.

## 7. Retention

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| CCTV video | 30 days, unless kept for an incident |
| Member Wi-Fi connection logs | 30 days |
| DinkDesk conversations | 12 months, with personal data masked |
| Closed membership records | As required by tax and accounting rules, then deleted |
