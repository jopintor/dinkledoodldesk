# Reading the IT Service Status Page

The status page at `status.picklehub.example` shows the live health of PickleHub's IT services. Anyone can view it without signing in. DinkDesk reads the same information when you ask "Is CourtBook down?" or "What is the status of the Wi-Fi in Imus?"

## What the colours and labels mean

| Status | Colour | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Operational** | Green | Working normally. If you have a problem, it is probably specific to you or your device; follow the troubleshooting guide or raise a ticket. |
| **Degraded Performance** | Yellow | Working but slow or with occasional errors. Keep using it; expect delays. |
| **Partial Outage** | Orange | Not working for some branches, some users or some features. Check the incident note to see who is affected. |
| **Major Outage** | Red | Not working for anyone. IT is already working on it. |
| **Under Maintenance** | Blue | Planned work. The service may be unavailable during the window shown. |

## Services listed

- CourtBook (bookings and CourtBook Admin)
- PickleHub Member App
- DinkPOS
- Card Payments (TapPay terminals)
- HubMail
- Staff Wi-Fi and Member Wi-Fi, listed per branch (DAS, IMS, BAC, GTR, TAG)
- HubConnect VPN
- HubWatch CCTV
- RallyBracket
- DinkDesk

## What to do when a service shows a problem

1. **Do not raise a duplicate ticket.** If the service you need already shows Partial Outage, Major Outage or Maintenance, IT already knows. Extra tickets slow the team down.
2. Read the incident note. It says which branches are affected, the workaround (for example, take cash while card payments are down) and the time of the next update.
3. Click **Subscribe** to get email updates for that service.
4. Raise a ticket only if your problem is **different** from the one described, or if the note says the issue is fixed but it still affects you.

## When the page is green but something is broken

The page is updated by IT when they confirm an incident, so a brand-new problem may not appear for 10 to 15 minutes. If several people at your branch are affected, raise a ticket right away so IT can confirm it.

## Planned maintenance

The regular maintenance window is **every Tuesday, 1:00 AM to 4:00 AM**, when all clubs are closed. Maintenance outside this window is announced on the status page at least 48 hours ahead, except for urgent security fixes.

## Incident history

The **History** tab shows past incidents for the last 90 days. Branch managers can use it when reviewing why sales or bookings were lower on a given day.
