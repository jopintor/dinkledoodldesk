# Acceptable Use Policy for IT Resources

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for using PickleHub Clubs' IT resources: accounts, devices (laptops, front-desk tablets, POS equipment), networks, email, business systems and DinkDesk. It applies to all employees, contractors and coaches who use these resources.

## 2. Accounts and passwords

1. Each person uses only their own accounts. Sharing StaffID, Cashier IDs, PINs or HubWatch accounts is not allowed, even for a single shift.
2. Keep passwords, PINs and MFA codes private. IT will never ask for them.
3. Lock or sign out of shared tablets and PCs when you step away.
4. Report suspected account misuse right away (KB-027).

## 3. Devices

1. Company devices are for work. Light personal use on a company laptop (for example, checking the news on a break) is fine if it does not interfere with work or break this policy.
2. Do not install software on company devices yourself. Ask IT through a ticket; IT checks licences and security first.
3. Do not connect unknown USB drives to POS tablets or office PCs.
4. Do not remove security software, change system settings or try to get administrator rights you were not given.
5. Report lost or stolen devices the same day.

## 4. Networks

1. Work devices connect to `PickleHub-Staff`. Do not connect personal or guest devices to `PickleHub-Devices`.
2. Do not set up your own Wi-Fi routers, extenders or hotspots for club equipment.
3. Do not try to bypass content filters or monitor other people's network traffic.

## 5. Email and messaging

1. Use HubMail for work communication with members and partners.
2. Do not forward work email containing member data to personal email accounts.
3. Do not send member lists, payment information or ID photos in personal group chats.

## 6. Generative AI tools

1. DinkDesk is the approved AI assistant for IT questions.
2. Do not paste member personal data, payment details, passwords or internal reports into public AI chatbots or other AI tools that IT has not approved.
3. AI answers can be wrong. Check important actions with IT or the relevant SOP.

## 7. Monitoring

PickleHub may log and review use of its systems (for example, sign-in logs, DinkPOS transaction logs, HubWatch access logs and DinkDesk conversations) to keep systems secure and investigate incidents. Monitoring is done in line with the Data Privacy Policy (KB-023) and only by authorised staff.

## 8. Breaking this policy

Breaches of this policy may lead to removal of access and disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct. Report possible breaches to your manager or to `security@picklehub.example`.
