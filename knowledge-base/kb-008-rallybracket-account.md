# RallyBracket Tournament Software Accounts

RallyBracket is the cloud software PickleHub uses to run tournaments and leagues: player registration lists, brackets, court assignments and live scoring. It is provided by an outside vendor and is **not** connected to StaffID.

## Who can have an account

| RallyBracket role | Who | Approval needed |
|---|---|---|
| Organizer | Events team | Events Coordinator |
| Referee / Scorer | Coaches and staff assigned to a tournament | Events Coordinator |
| Viewer | Branch managers | Events Coordinator |

Members and players do not get RallyBracket staff accounts. Players see brackets on the public tournament page or in the Member App.

## Requesting an account

1. Submit an access request (KB-010) and choose **RallyBracket** with the role you need.
2. The Events Coordinator approves or declines it.
3. After approval, you receive an invitation email from RallyBracket at your HubMail address.
4. Open the invitation within 7 days and create your RallyBracket password.

Referee and Scorer accounts are given for a specific tournament and expire 3 days after the event ends.

## Forgot RallyBracket password

Because RallyBracket is a separate service, reset it on the RallyBracket website:

1. Go to the RallyBracket sign-in page and click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your HubMail address.
3. Follow the reset link in the email. The link is valid for 1 hour.

If the reset email never arrives, check Spam, then ask DinkDesk to create a ticket. The Applications Specialist will contact the vendor.

DinkDesk's StaffID password reset does not work for RallyBracket.

## Account locked

RallyBracket locks accounts after repeated failed sign-ins. Wait 30 minutes or use **Forgot password**. If it is a tournament day and scoring is blocked, raise a P2 ticket and mention the tournament name and start time.

## Good practice

- Use a password different from your StaffID password.
- Do not share Organizer accounts between events staff.
- Player lists exported from RallyBracket contain personal data. Store them only in the shared Events folder in HubMail Drive, not on personal devices (KB-023).
