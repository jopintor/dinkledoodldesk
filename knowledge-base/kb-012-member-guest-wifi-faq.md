# Member and Guest Wi-Fi (PickleHub-Members) FAQ

Free Wi-Fi is available for members and guests at every PickleHub branch on the network named `PickleHub-Members`.

## How do I connect?

1. Choose `PickleHub-Members` in your phone's Wi-Fi list. There is no Wi-Fi password.
2. A sign-in page opens. If it does not, open any website and it will appear.
3. Members: tap **Sign in with PickleHub** and use your Member App email and password.
4. Guests: enter the **day-pass code** printed on your receipt or given by the front desk.

## How long does a session last?

- Members stay connected for **8 hours**, then sign in again.
- A guest day-pass code works for **4 hours** from first use.

## How many devices can I connect?

Each member account can have **2 devices** connected at the same time. Signing in on a third device disconnects the oldest one.

## The sign-in page does not appear.

1. Turn off any VPN or private DNS setting on your phone.
2. Open a plain website address such as `neverssl.com` in your browser.
3. Forget the `PickleHub-Members` network and join again.

## It says my member password is wrong.

The Wi-Fi uses your Member App login. If you cannot sign in to the Member App either, see KB-018 to reset your password. Your account may also be locked for 30 minutes after 5 wrong attempts.

## Connected but very slow.

Member Wi-Fi is shared by everyone at the club and is limited to about **10 Mbps per device**, which is enough for messaging, social media and streaming at normal quality. Large downloads and video calls may be slow at busy times (usually 6:00 – 9:00 PM).

## Can I use the staff Wi-Fi instead?

No. `PickleHub-Staff` is for employees' work devices only.

## Is the Wi-Fi secure?

`PickleHub-Members` is a public network. Avoid entering banking details on any public Wi-Fi unless the site uses HTTPS. PickleHub does not look at the content of your browsing, but it keeps basic connection logs (device ID and time) for security for 30 days, as described in the Member Privacy Notice.

## Who do I tell if the Wi-Fi is down?

Tell the front desk. Staff can check the status page and raise a ticket for the branch.
