# HubWatch CCTV Viewer Access

HubWatch is the software used to view live and recorded CCTV video from each branch's video recorder. CCTV video shows identifiable people, so it is personal data under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (see KB-023). Access is limited and logged.

## Who may have HubWatch access

| Person | Access | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Branch Manager | Live view and playback | Own branch only |
| Security Officer | Live view and playback | Assigned branches |
| Operations Head | Live view and playback | All branches |
| Systems and Network Administrator | Maintenance access | All branches, for technical work only |

Front-desk staff, coaches and members do **not** receive HubWatch accounts. If you need something checked on camera, ask your branch manager.

## Requesting access

1. Your request must come through an access request (KB-010), choosing **HubWatch CCTV** and the branch.
2. Approvals required: your **branch manager** (if you are not the manager) **and** the **Operations Head**.
3. The Data Protection Officer is notified automatically of every new HubWatch account.
4. The Systems and Network Administrator creates the account and sends sign-in instructions.

DinkDesk cannot grant or speed up CCTV access. It can only explain the process and log the request.

## Signing in

HubWatch uses its own username and password, separate from StaffID. Access works only from the branch office PC or through HubConnect VPN (KB-013).

## Forgot HubWatch password or account locked

Ask DinkDesk to create a ticket. The Systems and Network Administrator resets HubWatch passwords after confirming the request with you by phone.

## Requests for footage

Requests to **copy or export** video, or to show footage to anyone outside the approved list (for example a member, an insurer or the police), are **not** IT requests. Send them to the Data Protection Officer at `dpo@picklehub.example`. The DPO decides what can be released and how.

## Retention

Branch recorders keep video for **30 days**. After that it is overwritten automatically. If an incident needs footage kept longer, the branch manager must tell the DPO before the 30 days run out.

## Rules for viewers

- View footage only for security, safety or incident investigation.
- Do not record the screen with a phone or share clips in group chats.
- Every login and playback is logged and reviewed monthly by the Systems and Network Administrator.
